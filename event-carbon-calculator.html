🧭How is travel distance calculated?

For each journey, you only need to enter the straight-line distance.

Mode Correction applied to the straight-line distance
🚗 Car, carpool × 1.3
🚆 Train (TGV or Western Europe average) × 1.2
🚶 Walking, 🚲 cycling, ⛴️ ferry direct distance (no correction)
✈️ Flight + 95 km (flat)
Emissions of a journey = adjusted distance × emission factor of the mode (per passenger-km) × 2 if round trip

Ferry is not covered by the Labos1point5 methodology.

Source: Labos1point5 — travel simulator

Additional computation details: GitHub: README.md
